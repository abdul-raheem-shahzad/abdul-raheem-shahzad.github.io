About

I build machine-learning models for clinical and biomedical prediction. My current work, with Prof. Zhang's team at Zhongnan Hospital of Wuhan University, predicts live birth after IVF/ICSI from embryo morphology and structured clinical data.

I completed my M.S. in Software Engineering at Wuhan University as a China Scholarship Council scholar (GPA 3.67/4.0, ranked 2nd in class). I hold a B.S. in Software Engineering from CECOS University, where I graduated first in the department with a gold medal. Alongside clinical ML, I apply deep learning to environmental data, including groundwater forecasting and seagrass habitat modelling.
