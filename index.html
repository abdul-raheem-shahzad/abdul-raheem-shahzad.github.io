About

I am a machine learning engineer. I build, evaluate and deploy models for computer vision, NLP, generative modelling and time-series forecasting, and I have shipped them in healthcare, climate science, agriculture and urban monitoring.

I completed my M.S. in Software Engineering at Wuhan University as a China Scholarship Council scholar (GPA 3.67/4.0, ranked 2nd in class), and my B.S. at CECOS University, where I graduated first in the department with a gold medal. My work spans live-birth prediction for IVF/ICSI with Zhongnan Hospital, groundwater forecasting under climate change, seagrass habitat modelling, YOLOv7 detection of visual pollution, and benchmarking LLM-generated clinical summaries. I also taught data science and machine learning for two years and deploy my models as working dashboards.
